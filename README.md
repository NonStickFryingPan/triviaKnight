# triviaKnight

triviaKnight turns raw notes into flashcards, multiple choice questions and
fill-in-the-blank cards, then schedules review sessions that adapt to how well
you remember things. Paste a dump of facts, let one small AI call shape them
into cards, and quiz yourself on a rhythm tuned to your memory. Everything
stays in your browser, so you own your data. Open it on a laptop and a phone,
connect the two with a Google Sheet for backup and sync. No account, no server,
no install.
